Incaendo Technologies is a software development firm in Noida, founded in 2012, building web, mobile and AI products for startups, SMEs and enterprises, with clients in the UK, UAE, Indonesia, the US and Europe. Everything made for Incaendo – decks, proposals, documents, landing pages, product screens – uses this system so the brand reads the same everywhere: **caring, inclusive yet professional**.

## Brand in one line

Caring, inclusive and professional. Incaendo is orange-led: orange carries warmth and energy and marks the actions and highlights; navy carries weight and structure; warm light surfaces give calm and space. Plenty of white space, one idea per block, no decoration that does not explain something.

## Content fundamentals

**Tone of voice: caring, inclusive yet professional.** Incaendo is a close-knit team that works like a family, and it should sound like people who care about the reader – while staying clear, competent and businesslike.

- **Caring** – start from the reader's situation, not from Incaendo. Acknowledge what they are dealing with, then show how we will help. Offer support openly ("We're happy to walk your team through this").
- **Inclusive** – write for everyone in the room: founders, engineers, finance and end users. Plain language over jargon; explain a technical term the first time it appears. Use "we" and "together"; avoid assumptions about gender, culture or technical background.
- **Professional** – precise, structured and honest. Clear scope, clear commercials, clear next steps. Confident without boasting; no hype, no blame.

**Rules.**

- British English: "organisation", "optimise", "colour", "programme" (but "program" for software).
- Write "we" for Incaendo and "you" for the client. Name the client in titles: "Proposal for a customer portal – [Client name]".
- Sentence case for headings and buttons ("Scope of work", "Book a call"). Uppercase only for the `eyebrow` style.
- No emoji. No em dashes – use a spaced en dash ( – ). No exclamation marks in client documents.
- Numbers over adjectives: "500+ projects delivered", "building software since 2012", "₹2,50,000 + GST". Indian clients in ₹ with lakh grouping; international clients in USD or GBP.
- Lead with the outcome for the client, then how we deliver it. Avoid filler such as "cutting-edge", "best-in-class", "revolutionise".
- Every client message ends with a clear next step and an offer of help, then a short, warm sign-off: "Thank you," and the sender's name.

Good: "Running a clinic is hard enough. Nivaran keeps bookings, records and follow-ups in one place, so your team can spend more time with patients. We'd be glad to set it up with you."
Good: "Here is what we need from your side to start Phase 1, and how we will support your team at each step."
Avoid: "We are one of the best companies providing end-to-end cutting-edge solutions!"
Avoid: "As per the requirement, the client must provide the documents."

## Colour

Incaendo is **orange-led** (chosen November 2026 by a team vote). `orange` (#EE5D10) and `blue` (#145AA8) are taken exactly from the logo.

- Rough balance on any page or slide: 70% warm light surfaces (`canvas`, `surface`, `surface-raised`), 20% `navy`, 10% orange. Blue stays under 5%.
- **Actions:** primary buttons use `action` (#C24C12) with a white `on-action` label (4.84:1), darkening to `action-hover` (#A83F0A). Never put white text on the logo orange (#EE5D10, 3.4:1) and never use gradients for buttons.
- **Orange text:** eyebrows, links, highlighted words in headlines and icons use `orange-text` (#B5450B). The logo `orange` is for accents only: section bars, the i-dot, the "+" on stats, and display text 24px and larger.
- **Highlighted headlines:** a headline may put its second half in `orange-text` (class `inc-highlight`). One highlight per headline.
- **Surfaces:** websites and landing pages use the warm `canvas` (#F8F7F4); documents and slides stay on white `surface`. Panels use warm `surface-raised`; brand tints use `orange-soft`.
- **Navy** (#0F2136) carries weight: stats panels, call-to-action bands, footers, title and closing slides.
- **Blue** is supporting only: the keyboard focus ring, info messages (`blue-soft`), chart series 3 and the logo.
- Text is `ink` on light grounds; secondary text `ink-muted`. Links are `link` (`orange-text`).
- Status colours (`success`, `warning`, `danger`) always come with a word ("On track", "At risk", "Blocked").
- Charts: use `chart-1` to `chart-5` in order (action orange, navy, blue, light orange, grey). Five series at most, label series directly, prefer bars, no rainbow pies.
- States: hover darkens to `action-hover`, pressed to `action-pressed`; disabled controls use 45% opacity and never carry the only copy of information. Scrims behind modals and the mobile menu use `overlay`.
- Dark theme exists for product screens and dashboards. Decks and documents are light, with `navy` title and closing slides.

## Typography

Incaendo's official typeface is **Plus Jakarta Sans** (chosen 18 November 2026). It is a free Google Font, so everyone on the team and every tool can use it, and it is the font of the new incaendo.com website.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings and display – modern and geometric, with the same confident character as the logo. Styles `display`, `h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `eyebrow`. Weights 600, 700 and 800.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body text too – clear at small sizes in documents and on screen. Styles `body-lg`, `body`, `body-strong`, `small`, `caption`. Weights 400 and 600.
- **JetBrains Mono** for code, endpoints and commands in technical proposals (`code`).
- Install from fonts.google.com on every Incaendo laptop and add to Google Docs and Slides via "More fonts". Where they genuinely cannot be used (a client's Word template), fall back to **Arial** for both headings and body – the fallbacks are in the font stacks.
- One `display` per page. Do not go below `caption` (12px). Do not set paragraphs in bold weights (700 and above) or in all caps.
- Line length 60–80 characters for documents; left-aligned, never justified.
- **Print sizes** (Word, Google Docs, PDF): body 11pt, small text 9pt, captions 8pt, h3 13pt, h2 16pt, h1 22pt, cover title 32pt.
- **Web sizes:** `web-display` 64px on desktop and 36px on phones, `web-h1` 48px and 32px, `web-h2` 40px and 28px, `web-lead` 20px and 18px. Body text stays 16px on every screen; nothing below 12px.

## Layout and spacing

- Spacing steps `space-1` to `space-10` (4px base). Card padding `space-5`; gaps between blocks `space-6`; between sections `space-7`; page margins `space-8`; website sections `space-9` and the hero `space-10`.
- Radii: `radius-md` for buttons and cards, `radius-lg` for large panels, `radius-sm` for badges and tables, `radius-pill` only for tags.
- Prefer `surface-raised` to shadows. `shadow-1` is for cards that float on a busy area; `shadow-2` and `shadow-3` are for web elements only (see Web foundations).
- Focus: 2px solid `focus` ring with 2px offset on every interactive element.

## Decks (Slides)

- 16:9, light `surface` slides with a 48px margin. Logo top-left on the first slide only; the `incaendo-mark.png` small in the footer of the rest, with the slide number in `caption`.
- Title and closing slides: `navy` ground, reversed logo, `display` title in `on-navy`, one orange element (a bar or the dot motif).
- Content slides: `eyebrow` in `orange-text`, `h1` headline that states the point ("Everything a doctor needs, in one screen"), then content in tinted cards (`surface-raised` or `orange-soft`) on a grid.
- Case-study slides: client name and one-line outcome, a screenshot in a `radius-lg` frame, three facts (problem, what we built, result with a number), tech stack as `Badge`s.
- Standard Incaendo deck order: Title – About Incaendo – What we do – How we work (process) – Case studies – Clients – Next steps / contact.
- Avoid: textured dark gradients, clip-art, stock 3D devices, Wingdings bullets, rainbow charts, more than 40 words on a slide.

## Proposals and documents (Docs)

- Cover: primary logo top-left, then `eyebrow` "PROPOSAL", `h1` title, client name, country, contact person and date in a `surface-raised` panel with `radius-lg`.
- Standard section order: Executive summary – Understanding of requirement – Scope of work (by phase) – Deliverables – Timeline – Commercial summary – Terms and conditions – About Incaendo – Clients.

## Web foundations

For the website, landing pages and any web screen.

- **Breakpoints:** `bp-sm` 640px, `bp-md` 768px, `bp-lg` 1024px, `bp-xl` 1280px. Design phone-first: one column below `bp-md`, two from `bp-md`, three from `bp-lg`, four from `bp-xl` where the content suits it.
- **Containers:** content at most `container-content` (1200px) wide, reading text at most `container-text` (720px). Side padding is `gutter-mobile` (16px) on phones and `gutter` (24px) from `bp-md`. Classes: `inc-container`, `inc-container-text`, `inc-web-grid`.
- **Section rhythm:** sections are separated by `space-9` (96px) on desktop and `space-8` (64px) on phones (`inc-page-section`); the hero may use `space-10` (128px). Alternate `canvas` and `surface-raised` backgrounds to mark sections, with navy for one emphasis band per page.
- **Depth:** `shadow-1` for cards, `shadow-2` for dropdowns and the scrolled header, `shadow-3` for modals and the open mobile menu.
- **Motion:** `--motion-fast` (150ms) for hovers, `--motion-base` (250ms) for menus and accordions, `--motion-slow` (400ms) for scroll reveals, easing `--ease-out`. Content must be fully visible without animation, nothing auto-advances while someone is reading, and `prefers-reduced-motion` turns motion off (built into the stylesheet).

**Accessibility checklist** (every page, every component):

1. Anything tappable is at least `tap-min` (44px) tall and wide, including padding around text links and carousel dots.
2. Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for 24px and larger); use `orange-text` and `action`, never the logo orange, for text and buttons.
3. Visible blue focus ring on every interactive element; a skip link (`inc-skip-link`) at the top of each page.
4. Real `<button>`, `<a href>` and labelled form fields; everything works by keyboard.
5. Meaningful alt text on every logo and image; decorative images get empty alt.
6. One `h1` per page and headings in order.
7. Checked at 390px wide before sign-off.

## Components

Components are plain CSS classes in `components/bundle.css` (prefix `inc-`), loaded after `tokens.css`: `Button`, `Badge`, `Card`, `SectionHeader`, `Stat`, `Table`, `Callout`, and the web page shell: `SiteHeader`, `Hero`, `CtaBand`, `SiteFooter`. The page shell switches layout by the width of its `inc-shell` wrapper (container queries), so the same markup works in a full page and a narrow frame. See each component's guidelines.
