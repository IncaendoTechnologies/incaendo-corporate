# Logos

The current Incaendo logo (used on proposals since 2024). Full-colour PNGs with transparent backgrounds; they cannot be recoloured with CSS.

- `incaendo-logo.png` – primary logo, blue (#145AA8) "iNCAENDO" over orange (#EE5D10) "TECHNOLOGIES". On `surface`, `canvas` or `surface-raised` only. Minimum 32px tall on screen.
- `incaendo-logo-reversed.png` – white "iNCAENDO", orange "TECHNOLOGIES". On `navy` or dark photos only.
- `incaendo-mark.png` – the "iN" monogram with the orange dot. Favicons, app icons, slide footers, avatars; on light grounds.
