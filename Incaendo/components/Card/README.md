# Card

A card groups one idea: a service, a feature, a benefit or a case-study fact. The consumer provides a `<div class="inc-card">` (or an `<a>` when the whole card links) containing an optional `inc-card-icon` with a Lucide icon, an `inc-card-title` (h3) and an `inc-card-text` paragraph of one or two sentences.

- `inc-card` – warm `surface-raised` panel. The default; lay cards in a grid of 3 (or 2 on narrow pages) with `space-5` gutters. The icon sits in an `orange-soft` circle in `orange-text`.
- `inc-card-tint` – `orange-soft`: the one highlighted card in a set.
- `inc-card-navy` – the "result" or summary card that closes a set (one per set), with an orange icon.
- `inc-card-elevated` – white card with `shadow-1`, on the warm canvas or a busy area.
- `inc-card-blue` – info only (rare); not for highlighting.

Do: keep titles to five words and text to two lines. Don't: add coloured left borders, emoji, or more than one navy card per grid.
