# Stat

A single proof number with a short label, used in rows of three or four on About slides, landing pages and proposal "About Incaendo" sections. The consumer provides `<div class="inc-stat">` with an `inc-stat-value` (wrap a suffix such as "+" in `<em>` to colour it orange) and an `inc-stat-label`.

Use only the approved company figures: 500+ projects delivered, building software since 2012, a team of 50+, and clients in the UK, UAE, Indonesia, the US and Europe. Client-specific results belong on case-study slides, with the client's approval. Keep labels short.
